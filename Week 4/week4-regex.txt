ls -R | grep -Eio "\.[a-zA-Z]{3,4}\b" | sort | uniq -c | sort -nr

ls -R
Lists files and directories.
-R means recursive

| pipe

Flags:
-E use extended regex
-i case-insensitive
-o only print the matched part

-c
shows how many times each extension appeared

-n
numeric sort

-r
reverse (largest first)
